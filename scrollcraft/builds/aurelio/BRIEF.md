Two clusters dominate and they agree with each other:

- **Late-90s / 00s action anime**: Cowboy Bebop, Black Lagoon, Soul Eater, Chainsaw Man,
  FMA Brotherhood. Hard cuts, bold graphic title cards, jazz, a skull mascot. Bebop is the
  spine: it is literally a jazz anime, which is what the surviving jazzskull already is.
- **N64-era games**: Donkey Kong 64, Mario Kart 64, Smash 64, Minecraft. Low-poly, chunky
  vertex-lit geometry, flat saturated color, no post-processing.

Cartoon Network and Looney Tunes push the same way: hard graphic shapes, bumper energy.
King Crimson, Pink Floyd and Tame Impala supply the prog-psych color. Goodfellas supplies
the confidence, not the palette.

## The peak

**"It's the site where you get to fly his drone."**

Lives in act 9, and holds from act 8 to the end of the page: once the controls are handed
over they are never taken back, so the drone stays yours through the kitchen and the close.
That hold is what gives the peak its scroll room without pinning any act.

## Authored silence

Act 3, act 7 and act 11 are near-empty by design: one word on a flat ground, no image, no
body copy, under a viewport each. They are the hard cuts, and the verification pass must not
